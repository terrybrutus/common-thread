The service verifies mainnet query signatures against a fixed configured backend. Legacy native Sites login/popups are only for the original private-beta owner’s restore and visitor tools. Keep `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` only in hosted secret storage or ignored local `.env.local`. Never push keys, personal recovery data or service bypass tokens or put them in browser variables. Do not resume Caffeine Inference. Keep authentication, origin checks, input/output bounds, no automatic retries and local in-flight protection. Preserve historical ledger rows. All-personal and surprise/together are the defaults. Personal terms require source quotations; optional mix allows at most one themed group. One optional participant-name pair serves both puzzle attribution and invitations; send it only for personal generation. No duplicate nickname fields. First-person story text belongs to the creator; normalize minor name typos only with unique corroboration. Never rewrite supporting quotations. Keep the quiet typing path and portfolio footer credit.

Literal tiles may use a complete shorter phrase only when it occurs at word boundaries inside both the original source and its quoted evidence; the full source/quote must still be grounded in the story. Never permit arbitrary within-word deletions or unsupported abbreviations. Exact source spellings remain valid even if a nearby participant name suggests a possible typo. Draft-validation failures should describe a rejected AI draft, preserve saved answers, and offer the existing generation control rather than implying the user must edit their story.

Personal story drafts may use literal, reverse, fill-blank, first-word and last-word. First/last-word sources are complete meaningful two-word names/titles, never slices of longer names, and must share the IDENTICAL omitted word. Removing different words from unrelated titles/places is not a connection. Do not force wordplay or auto-reverse parents to meet a quota; a coherent factual personal board may pass at any difficulty. Personal boards get one bounded independent whole-board audit before release; this additional call never regenerates a draft. Compute declared reversals from source terms, then run the unchanged strict provenance validator. Do not count common grammar words as a meaningful personal group. Keep no automatic paid retries.

Keep the optional freshness question spoiler-free and skippable; persist dismissal for unchanged story content and preserve existing answers. No extra model calls or automatic paid retries. History syncs on explicit cloud save or next generation, not live between friends. Preserve original relationship labels/explanations when applying deterministic wordplay.

## Accounts and daily play

- Ordinary story save/load requires the Google-linked game account and must never fall back to a native ChatGPT/Sites popup. Keep the explicit original-owner restore/visitor path separate. Local previews save on the device and offer JSON backups; do not advertise a private-cloud button there. Put visible Import story (.json) and Download story (.json) controls together; validate imports and preserve existing stories on failure.

- Guided story review must open the saved-answer disclosure when explicitly requested. Skipping the last added prompt advances to the next specific unused suggestion while space remains, and keeps the editor open with a clear end notice when exhausted. Do not suggest generic “What new memory…” questions; retain previously answered retired prompts as history, but omit unanswered retired prompts when loading.

- Personal puzzles can describe one life, someone the creator knows, or any group. Persist storyScope through backup/cloud/request validation and pass it to generation and review. New stories start with My life and hints; preserve existing answers and explicit settings. Never assume a romantic partner or exactly two people. Adapt prompts, optional subject/group name labels, and AI voice to the chosen scope. Direct Play now bypasses sharing; Share puzzle remains optional. Keep learning on its dedicated route rather than adding a second puzzle-type picker inside personal settings. Connected stories sync automatically; backup import/export remains available without a cloud popup.

- Never seed Terry’s private story into another account or automatically import unscoped beta browser data. Scope every story/topic read and write by the verified caller. Browser caches use ct-account:<principal>: keys, never auth secrets. Keep personal stories entirely out of learning context. Both learning topics must explicitly opt in before connecting; progress saves must preserve the stored consent. Account deletion keeps a minimal tombstone and a generation fence against stale-device restoration.
- Keep ready-made/daily play anonymous. A deterministic daily board based on the player’s local calendar date mixes reviewed catalog groups; it is a daily combination, not a claim of 365 entirely new groups. Saved user data and AI generation require sign-in in deployed builds.
- Visitor email is optional, self-reported and separately opted in, never automatically collected from Google or treated as marketing consent. Owner visitor exports have aggregate signed-in counts and opted-in emails, never story contents. Do not claim legal compliance across jurisdictions or count anonymous visitors as known users.

- Reveal explanations use ordinary sentence case, preserving proper names/acronyms. Wordplay restores all four names/words and stops; factual reveals give one shared explanation or balanced coverage of all four. No unrelated single-item trivia, off-board alternatives, exclusion commentary or ritual disclaimers. Fix reviewed catalog copy directly as well as generation guidance; never blanket-lowercase source/evidence text.

- Incorrect guesses highlight and briefly shake the four rejected tiles, show an explicit remaining-mistake notice, and pulse the counter. A repeated guess never spends another mistake or triggers another vibration. Respect reduced motion; keep visual/text feedback when vibration is absent/blocked. Allow the final rejected selection to finish before showing answers, and disable controls during that short transition. Do not add sound, automatic retries or generation calls for game feedback.

- Daily puzzles follow the device’s local calendar date and become available at local midnight. Archive dates use calendar-day subtraction, not 24-hour durations, to survive DST. Freeze an active round’s date until the player starts another round; do not replace a board in progress at midnight.

- Keep Common Thread’s own favicon, social preview and supported login app metadata. Do not restore Caffeine promotional assets. Minify production bundles and keep source maps disabled, without claiming this hides browser code. Do not silently change the identity derivation origin or swap authentication providers: existing user IDs/data need a verified migration. Internet Identity remains the actual signer until direct Google OAuth is configured and end-to-end tested.
